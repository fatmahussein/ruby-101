 Integers are whole numbers, such as 10. Floats are numbers that contain a decimal point

# arithmetic with two integers in Ruby, the result will always be an integer
17 / 5  #=> 3

# To obtain an accurate answer, just replace one of the integers in the expression with a float.
17 / 5.0 # => 3.4

# To convert a float to an integer:
13.0.to_i #=> 13

# To convert an integer to a float:
13.to_f #=> 13.0

6.even? #=> true
6.odd? #=> false
